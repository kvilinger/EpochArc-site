# Inside Step 5 Preview: Architecture and Training for Agentic Work
...
Step 5 Preview was designed with these constraints in mind. Architecturally, it follows a narrow-but-deep design with 92 transformer blocks. It interleaves Gated Sliding Window Attention with sparse full-attention layers, with Sparse GQA used in the full-attention path to support efficient long-context training and inference. Scaling this design also surfaced stability issues in deep training, leading us to revisit numerical precision and gradient handling.
