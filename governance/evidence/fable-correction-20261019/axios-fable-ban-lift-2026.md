Title: Trump administration lifts restrictions on Anthropic's Fable 5

URL Source: https://www.axios.com/2026/06/30/trump-anthropic-ai-model-fable-restrictions

Published Time: 2026-06-30T23:44:22.077392Z

Markdown Content:
The [Trump](https://www.axios.com/politics-policy/donald-trump) administration lifted export controls on Anthropic's Claude Fable 5 AI model Tuesday evening, with access returning to customers Wednesday, Anthropic said.

**Why it matters:**The move, eagerly awaited by AI developers, restores public access to the company's [powerful Mythos-class model](https://www.axios.com/2026/06/09/anthropic-mythos-class-safeguards) that had been pulled for security reasons 18 days ago.

**Driving the news:** Last week, the Trump administration allowed Anthropic to [restore access to Mythos 5](https://www.axios.com/2026/06/27/commerce-anthropic-mythos-restrictions-lift) for a select group of government-approved organizations.

*   Sources also [told](https://www.axios.com/2026/06/27/anthropic-fable-5-return-soon) Axios last week that Fable 5 could return as soon as this week.

**The big picture:** The U.S. government's desired role in regulating and evaluating frontier AI models before release is still up in the air — creating an ad hoc regulatory environment for AI companies.

*   OpenAI rolled out [GPT-5.6](https://www.axios.com/2026/06/26/openai-gpt-sol-terra-luna-trump) to only a small set of approved customers last week after a [request](https://www.axios.com/2026/06/25/trump-administration-openai-gpt-model-release) from the U.S. government. Anthropic's Mythos 5 is similarly being [released to a group](https://www.axios.com/2026/06/27/commerce-anthropic-mythos-restrictions-lift) of government-approved customers. 

**What they're saying:** Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick [said](https://x.com/howardlutnick/status/2072100729603452965?s=46&t=lWeRdBU5epOoS27GJ8FHHg) Tuesday in a post on X that his office had "worked closely with Anthropic to analyze and approve Fable 5 to ensure alignment across the US Government and strengthen America's leadership in AI."

*   "We're grateful to our users for their patience, and to everyone who worked with us on redeploying the models," Anthropic [said](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2072106151890809341) on X. 
*   White House chief of staff Susie Wiles [wrote](https://x.com/SusieWiles47/status/2072099604481335711) on X that the government and private sector have "worked together in a way we have never seen before and this foundation of America First is unprecedented." 

**Zoom in**:Anthropic says that it worked closely with the government to implement a new safeguard that blocks the jailbreak that officials were worried about 99% of the time.

*   In the other cases, Fable's output only included previously discovered or already patched security flaws, the company said. 
*   However, the new safeguard could result in Fable flagging more benign requests.
*   Commerce's Center for AI Standards and Innovation also tested the model's previous and new safeguards, according to Anthropic. 

**Threat level:**U.S. officials, allies and leading AI companies have become increasingly concerned that frontier models could be misused to automate sophisticated cyberattacks or accelerate biological weapons development.

*   At the same time, China is moving closer to [producing its own Mythos competitor](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/chinas-360-says-it-has-developed-tools-match-anthropics-mythos-2026-06-24/) while continuing to release [open-weight models](https://www.axios.com/2026/06/25/china-glm-52-open-source-hackers) that rival models like Anthropic's Claude Opus 4.8.

**What's next:** The Trump administration faces an August deadline under a [recent executive order](https://www.axios.com/2026/06/02/trump-signs-new-ai-executive-order) to create standardized benchmarks for evaluating the security risks of new AI models.
