An Anthropic risk assessment from AI safety organization METR is out.

Among
other findings
, METR says that Anthropic is “likely improving the capabilities of their models on well-specified tasks at around twice the rate they did between March 2024 and October 2025,” “likely” thanks to the “extensive” use of AI for R&D.

METR was in communication with Anthropic on this report starting in mid-August, and it submitted the final public report on November 3rd; in mid-September, CEO Dario Amodei
published
his
“We Must Pace the Frontier”
essay. Anthropic co-founder Sam McCandlish
says that
the company plans to share “a report with our own analysis of the speed of AI progress within Anthropic.”

Update
: Added context from McCandlish
.
