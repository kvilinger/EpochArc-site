The UK is formally investigating X over Grok deepfakes.

Ofcom says the probe will establish whether X has failed to comply with Online Safety Act obligations, over concerns its Grok AI chatbot is generating [sexualized deepfakes of adults and minors](https://www.theverge.com/news/853191/grok-explicit-bikini-pictures-minors). The investigation is “a matter of the highest priority,” and may result in hefty fines or even [X being banned in the UK](https://www.theverge.com/news/859107/uk-prime-minister-x-ai-grok-deepfakes).

[Ofcom launches investigation into X over Grok sexualised imagery](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/online-safety/illegal-and-harmful-content/ofcom-launches-investigation-into-x-over-grok-sexualised-imagery)
