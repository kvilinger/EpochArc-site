# Just the Docs: Eight States Sue OpenAI, Alleging It Misled the Public About ChatGPT’s Safety

By [Matthew Nagle](https://www.justsecurity.org/author/naglematthew/ "Profile and articles by Matthew Nagle")

Published on October 15, 2026

The attorneys general of[Arizona](https://www.justsecurity.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Arizona.pdf),[Arkansas](https://www.justsecurity.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Arkansas.pdf),[Idaho](https://www.justsecurity.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Idaho.pdf),[Iowa](https://www.justsecurity.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Iowa.pdf),[Kansas](https://www.justsecurity.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Kansas.pdf),[Montana](https://www.justsecurity.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Montana.pdf),[Nebraska](https://www.justsecurity.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Nebraska.pdf), and[West Virginia](https://www.justsecurity.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/West-Virginia.pdf) filed coordinated lawsuits against OpenAI in their own state courts on Oct. 14. The suits bring claims under state consumer protection laws, alleging that OpenAI marketed ChatGPT as safe while concealing its risks. They also present those risks as a threat to public safety.
