## The AI that _really_ does things.

Organizes your inbox, sends emails, manages your calendar, checks you in for flights.  
 All from WhatsApp, Telegram, or any chat app you already use.  
 Run it for yourself — or share it with your team.

No subscription. No hosted tier. No token. Stewarded by an independent 501(c)(3).  
Your memories. Your skills. Your models. Your machines. Your data.

Desktop Apps

Full apps that install everything for you — gateway, chat, setup, and node features. Available for macOS, Windows, and Linux.

macOS

macOS 15+ · Apple Silicon or Intel · v2026.10.4

Works on macOS, Linux, and Windows. The apps and the one-liner install Node.js and everything else for you. Switch later with `openclaw update --channel dev` or `openclaw update --channel stable`.

## Stay in the Loop. Get updates on new features, integrations, and lobster wisdom.

OpenClaw is stewarded by the [OpenClaw Foundation](https://openclaw.org/), an independent US 501(c)(3) non-profit. The Foundation employs the core team, signs every release, and keeps the whole product MIT licensed. There is no enterprise edition and no paid version.

The Foundation is funded by donors, including the University of Michigan, OpenAI, Amazon, Red Hat, Offline Holdings, and Lobster Computer Company. Donors fund the Foundation. They do not own, control, or direct the project, and no lab's model is privileged in the code. OpenAI is a donor and Peter Steinberger works there; OpenClaw is not an OpenAI product.

We have nothing to sell you, so we have no reason to route you anywhere. By default an install sends the Foundation nothing but a daily version check, and you can turn that off: [what OpenClaw sends](https://docs.openclaw.ai/gateway/telemetry).
