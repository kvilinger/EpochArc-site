You can now set up Gemini’s Google Wallet integration for ‘personal finance insights’

Abner Li
| Nov 13 2026 - 9:51 am PT

11 Comments

As we
spotted in September
, the Gemini app is
adding
Google Wallet integration in the US that makes possible “responses informed by your spending habits.”

In the Gemini app, @Google Wallet allows you to:

Visualize spending
: Questions like “can we review my spending from last quarter so I can save $1,000 over the next three months?” enable Gemini to generate dynamic charts tracking cash flow, asset allocation, and budgets directly in your chat.

Find forgotten subscriptions
: If you ask “what subscriptions am I still paying for, and did any of them go up in price this year?” Gemini finds recurring charges across your linked accounts and flags price increases.

Budget for the holidays
: When you ask “how much did I spend on gifts and travel last year? Use that to help me set a holiday budget for this year,” Gemini will look at your transactions from the last year and suggest a budget tailored to you.

The interactive visualizations (seen above) are quite cool. This integration leverages third-party service Plaid to “securely link your financial accounts from your credit cards and banks” to Google Wallet.

On the privacy front, this integration is read-only, with Gemini not able to “move, manage, or invest your money.”

Once enabled, @Google Wallet is available in the Gemini app on Android, iOS, and web for US users. To get started, go to Customize > Connectors or
gemini.google.com/customize/connectors
.

Google bills this as an expansion of Personal Intelligence, which already taps into Gmail, Photos, Search, and YouTube to customize responses. The company today also highlighted the launch of
Memory Summary
from earlier this week.
