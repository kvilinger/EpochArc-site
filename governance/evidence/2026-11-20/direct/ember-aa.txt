Muse Ember (Max) is amongst the leading models in intelligence and well priced when comparing to other models of similar price. It's also quicker than average per task, however somewhat verbose. The model supports text and image input, outputs text, and has a 1M tokens context window.

Muse Ember (Max) scores 36 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, placing it well above average among comparable models (median: 21). When evaluating the Intelligence Index, it generated 115M tokens, which is somewhat verbose in comparison to the median of 90M.

Pricing for Muse Ember (Max) is $0.10 per 1M input tokens (competitively priced, median: $0.22) and $0.50 per 1M output tokens (competitively priced, median: $0.81). On average, it costs $0.14 per task to evaluate Muse Ember (Max) on the Intelligence Index.

At 3.0 minutes per Intelligence Index task, Muse Ember (Max) is quicker than average compared with the median of 6.5 minutes for comparable models (lower is better).

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Muse Ember

When was Muse Ember released?

Muse Ember was released on November 9, 2026.

Who created Muse Ember?

Muse Ember was created by Meta.

How intelligent is Muse Ember?

Muse Ember has 2 effort levels: max and xhigh. Muse Ember scores 36 (max) and 34 (xhigh) on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index. Muse Ember (Max) is well above average among other models in a similar price tier (median: 21).

How fast is Muse Ember?

Muse Ember takes 3.0 minutes (max) and 2.8 minutes (xhigh) per Intelligence Index task (based on Meta's API; lower is better). Muse Ember (Max) is quicker than average compared to other models in a similar price tier (median: 6.5 minutes).

What is the latency of Muse Ember?

Muse Ember has a time to first answer token of 5.07s (max) and 3.66s (xhigh) (based on Meta's API). Muse Ember (Max)'s time to first answer token is better than average compared to other models in a similar price tier (median: 8.09s).

How much does Muse Ember cost?

Muse Ember costs $0.10 per 1M input tokens (very competitive, median: $0.22) and $0.50 per 1M output tokens (very competitive, median: $0.81), based on Meta's API.

What is Muse Ember API pricing?

Muse Ember costs $0.10 per 1M input tokens and $0.50 per 1M output tokens (based on Meta's API). For a blended rate (7:2:1 cache hit/input/output ratio), this is $0.0728 per 1M tokens. Pricing may vary by provider.
Compare provider pricing

How verbose is Muse Ember?

When evaluated on the Intelligence Index, Muse Ember generated 115M output tokens (max) and 90M output tokens (xhigh). Muse Ember (Max)'s output token count is somewhat higher than average compared to other models in a similar price tier (median: 90M).

Is Muse Ember a reasoning model?

Yes, Muse Ember is a reasoning model. It uses extended thinking or chain-of-thought reasoning to work through complex problems before providing an answer.

What input modalities does Muse Ember support?

Muse Ember supports text and image input.

What output modalities does Muse Ember support?

Muse Ember supports text output.

Can Muse Ember process images?

Yes, Muse Ember supports image input and can analyze, describe, and answer questions about images.

Is Muse Ember multimodal?

Yes, Muse Ember is multimodal. It can process text and image input and generate text output.

What is the context window of Muse Ember?

Muse Ember has a context window of 1M tokens. This determines how much text and conversation history the model can process in a single request.

Is Muse Ember open source?

No, Muse Ember is proprietary. The model weights are not publicly available.

How many parameters does Muse Ember have?

Muse Ember is a proprietary model and Meta has not disclosed the model size or parameter count.

How does Muse Ember perform on benchmarks?

Muse Ember achieves a score of 36 (max) and 34 (xhigh) on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index. This composite benchmark evaluates models across knowledge work, coding, science, and general knowledge.

Is Muse Ember available via API?

Yes, Muse Ember is available via API through 1 provider.
