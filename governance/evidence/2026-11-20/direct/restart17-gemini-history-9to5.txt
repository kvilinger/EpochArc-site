New Gemini setting lets you keep chat history, but turn off AI training

Abner Li
| Nov 16 2026 - 3:22 pm PT

8 Comments

Google is rolling out new privacy settings for the Gemini app that will let you “keep your history while independently controlling generative AI model training.”

Gemini’s existing
Keep Activity
setting “controls how long your chats are saved and whether they are used to train generative AI models.”

Google is now separating that preference into two new options.

The
first setting is called “History”
and lets you “control how long your history is saved.” If disabled:

Advertisement - scroll for more content

“…future chats will be saved for around 2 months and used to respond to you, process your feedback, and protect Google, its users, and the public, with help from human reviewers.”

The duration is
currently
24 hours for chat context, and 72 hours to help protect Google, users, and the public.

The
second setting
allows you to “separately control whether your chat content, including text and attachments, is used to train generative AI models.”

With this change, you will be able to keep your history while independently controlling generative AI model training.

If Keep Activity is currently off, the two “new settings will both be off.” These changes are rolling out over the “coming weeks,” and Google is notifying users via email.
