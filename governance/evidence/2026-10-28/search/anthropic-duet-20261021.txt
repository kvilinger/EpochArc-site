Anthropic on October 21, 2026 opened a research preview of [Claude Duet](https://claude.com/resources/articles/claude-duet), an app the company said turns Claude into a proactive agent that surfaces items needing attention, completes requested tasks, keeps working when users step away, and checks before acting on their behalf. Access runs through a waitlist, starting with Pro and Max users in the US.

Anthropic said it built Claude Duet for professionals and small business owners with more on their plates than they can track. The app is available as a standalone app on iOS and Android and on claude.ai; during the research preview it is included in Pro and Max plans and uses the same usage limits as the rest of Claude. Anthropic said it will email waitlisted users as soon as their access is ready.

## How Claude Duet Works

Anthropic said it designed Claude Duet around [Claude Opus 5.5](https://www.unite.ai/anthropic-releases-claude-opus-5-5-with-lower-pricing-and-new-safeguards/) and built it to decide what needs the user, what can wait, and when to check first. Each morning the app sends a short brief on the most important items across the user’s connected apps; during the day it keeps tabs on those apps and messages only when something needs attention. Tasks range from scheduling meetings to building dashboards with dozens of tools, creating financial or sales forecasts, prototyping, and building web apps.

Claude Duet carries over the memory, skills, and connected apps from a user’s Claude account, and users message it by typing or voice in one ongoing thread. The app runs on a virtual machine with its own browser, so it does not need the user’s laptop or phone to be on; it sends a notification when a task is complete or needs review. Anthropic said the app checks connected accounts every few hours because nonstop checking would consume plan limits much faster; those background checks count toward the shared usage limits, and larger tasks such as building a financial model or a deck require more model turns.

## Approval Gates, Connectors, and Payments

Claude Duet reads, drafts, and prepares work on its own, but before it sends a message, makes a payment, signs a document, or deletes anything, it shows the user exactly what it is about to do and waits for approval. A few actions are off-limits even with approval, including trading securities and moving money between accounts, and users can disconnect any app Claude Duet can access.

The app can use the more than 6,000 connectors and plugins in the Claude Marketplace, with Anthropic’s listed uses spanning help finding and replying to missed Gmail and Slack messages, unpaid-invoice checks in Intuit QuickBooks or Stripe with drafted reminders, preparing and running payroll in Gusto with hours, bonuses, and reimbursements filled in, and LinkedIn job postings published after the user edits and approves them. Other listed uses include routing agreements through Docusign for review and signature, drafting follow-up emails from HubSpot contact and deal history, turning a product launch into a Canva social campaign, and setting up, designing, and managing a Shopify store, with a richer visual storefront editor coming soon.

For business purchases, Claude Duet works with Link, Stripe’s digital wallet: the app confirms the item with the user, Link asks the user to approve the spend, and payment runs through a virtual card so the real card number is not shared. It can also use Shop Pay from Shopify to check out at online stores, and Anthropic said that, with user permission, it will soon be able to sign in to sites with logins saved in 1Password.

## Safety Design and Testing

Anthropic said Claude Duet draws on its framework for trustworthy agents, which also guides Claude Code and Claude in Chrome, and that its credential handling follows the design used for Claude Managed Agents, so the app never directly sees user passwords or sign-in tokens. The company said actions are approved by the auto mode classifier, the same safeguard that protects claude.ai and Claude Code from taking steps users did not request, and that the app’s defenses against hidden instructions and prompt injection come from its browser-use research. Anthropic said red teams and an external firm penetration tested Claude Duet, and the app is covered by its public bug bounty program on HackerOne.

Anthropic released Claude Duet with a new dedicated classifier and safety system prompt that checks each message, which the company said is designed to reject requests to share the strictest tier of information, including credit card numbers and government identification. Other sensitive information, such as health or personal financial details, passes only when the user has explicitly instructed Claude Duet to send it, Anthropic said. The company said it will share more details about the app’s safety design in the coming days.

## What Is Claude Duet, Exactly?

Claude Duet is described by Anthropic as a research preview of Claude that users message the way they would message a person, rather than typing prompts into a chat window and waiting for a reply. The product ships as standalone apps for iOS and Android, alongside a web version at claude.ai, giving it the same multi-platform footprint that rival products have used to drive adoption.

What separates Duet from the standard Claude chat experience is persistence. Instead of waiting for a prompt, the agent proactively surfaces what it judges needs the user’s attention, works through connected apps, and keeps operating on its own cloud-based computer even after the user has closed the app entirely. That last point is the technical crux of the “always-on agent” category: the work does not pause just because the device did.

Anthropic is positioning human review as a core boundary.
