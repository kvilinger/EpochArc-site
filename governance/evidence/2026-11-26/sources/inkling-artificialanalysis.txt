Thinking Machines has released Inkling 2 Mini, an open weights looped transformer
See model page
Thinking Machines has released Inkling 2 Mini at 20 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, an open weights looped transformer on the Pareto frontier for Intelligence vs. Active Parameters
Inkling 2 Mini is a 26B total, 5.4B active parameter mixture-of-experts model. The 5.4B active parameter count includes three passes through its looped layers; it uses 3.1B unique weights per text token, and it is small enough to run on a MacBook or DGX Spark.
It is released under an Apache 2.0 license, with text and image input and a 1M token context window.
Inkling 2 Mini matches Qwen3.8 27B (xhigh) and Nanbeige4.3-33B on the Intelligence Index with ~5x and ~6x fewer active parameters, respectively.
It is on the Pareto frontier for Intelligence vs. size on both total and active parameters.
Inkling 2 Mini uses ~38% more output tokens than Qwen3.8 27B for the same Intelligence Index score, but ~2.8x less compute.
Twelve of its 24 layers run three times per token, adding compute without adding output tokens. It averages ~152K output tokens per Intelligence Index task vs. ~110K for Qwen3.8 27B and ~158K for Nanbeige4.3-33B. On our compute proxy measure (active parameters × tokens), it sits on the Pareto frontier, using ~2.8x less compute than Qwen3.8 27B and ~5x less than Nanbeige4.3-33B.
Inkling 2 Mini is ~6x cheaper and ~4x faster per task than Qwen3.8 27B.
It costs $0.59 and takes 9.0 minutes per Intelligence Index task vs. $3.53 and 38.2 minutes for Qwen3.8 27B. GPT-6 Luna (max) scores 28 for $0.15 per task. Cost uses Thinking Machines' first-party pricing ($0.15/$0.50/$0.015 per 1M input/output/cache hit tokens).
