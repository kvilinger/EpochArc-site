OpenRouter Drops 3 Providers After Researchers Catch Months of Prompt Theft at AI Hosting Firms
Nov 22, 2026
Artificial Intelligence / Data Breach
Attackers stole data from inside three AI inference providers for months, and none of the three knew until researchers told them, the industrial cybersecurity company Dragos said this week. Some of the stolen requests were flagged as Zero Data Retention , the setting that is supposed to mean a prompt is never stored. OpenRouter , a gateway that routes customers' AI requests to a pool of third-party providers, said today that it has removed three providers from its platform, removed 125 endpoints from eligibility for Zero Data Retention, and notified all potentially affected customers. The findings showed no breach of OpenRouter itself, the company said. The earliest of the three breaches began in mid-July. The planted prompts were bait, but they were processed alongside real customer requests, and those were almost certainly copied too, the firm said.
