Google's Gemini 4 Neon evaluated across our benchmark suite
We evaluated Google’s new Gemini 4 Neon across our benchmark suite.
Neon ranks #2 on the Vals Index with 70.87%, at $7.40 per test.
It ranks #1 on the Vals Finance Index (68.32%, $4.88/test) and Vals Legal Index (78.36%, $6.10/test). It scores 62.63% on the Vals Science Index at $8.26/test.
In finance and law, Neon scores 76.46% on Earnings Modeling Bench v1.1, 68.56% on Finance Agent Bench v2.1, 56.25% on Legal Research Bench, 54.89% on Tax Agent Bench, and 19.58% on Harvey’s Legal Agent Benchmark.
In science and coding, it scores 93.70% on Vibe Code Bench, 80.00% on BioMysteryBench, 62.86% on Terminal-Bench Science, 61.62% on Terminal-Bench 4.0, and 45.05% on MysteryMechanism.
Neon is on the score–cost Pareto frontier on 7 of its 14 leaderboards: the Vals, Finance, and Legal indexes, Earnings Modeling Bench v1.1 ($3.82/test), Legal Research Bench ($7.37/test), Tax Agent Bench ($4.95/test), and Vibe Code Bench ($6.76/test). On those boards, no model with a reported cost scores at least as well for less or scores better for the same cost.
Pricing is $3 per million input tokens, $15 per million output tokens, and $0.15 per million cache-read tokens.
