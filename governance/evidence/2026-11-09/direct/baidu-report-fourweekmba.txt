Baidu Opens ERNIE 5.5 Flash Weights, Free Up to $50M Revenue

AI Business Brief
/ By
Gennaro Cuofano
/
November 6, 2026

Baidu has published the weights of ERNIE-5.5-Flash-Base, a Mixture-of-Experts
model
with “280B total parameters and 14B activated per token”, on
Hugging Face
. Its
license
lets companies use it commercially without a
model
license charge while annual
revenue
of the related products or services is at or below US$50 million.

Above US$50 million, commercial use
needs
Baidu’s separate written authorization, the license says. It is dated November 5, 2026. By our arithmetic, 14B of 280B means 5.0% of the parameters are active for each token.

, with 14B active per token (5.0% by our arithmetic), with a license that is free for commercial use up to US$50 million of related annual
revenue
and bars using its outputs to improve other AI models.

What Baidu Released

The
model
card says the
model
was “pre-trained from scratch on 33.5T tokens” with a training context of up to 128K, and that “fine-tuning and inference support a 1M-token context”. It is the checkpoint at the end of pre-training: the card calls it “the checkpoint at the end of pre-training, before any mid- or post-training”.

Baidu lists 43 layers, 512 routed experts per MoE layer (top-10) plus one shared expert, and a vocabulary of 201,216 tokens. FP8 and BF16 weights are both published.

What Baidu’s Benchmarks Show

Baidu reports its own results against three similar-sized base models, all run through what it calls “the same pipeline, prompts and few-shot settings”. On MMLU-Pro it lists 78.4 for ERNIE against 69.2 for DeepSeek-V4-Flash-Base, 9.2 points higher by our arithmetic. On LiveCodeBench it lists 40.1 against 31.9.

ERNIE does not lead every row in Baidu’s table. It lists DeepSeek-V4-Flash-Base ahead on ChineseSimpleQA (75.3 vs 73.0) and InfiniteBench (48.0 vs 46.5), and Hy3-preview-Base ahead on FullStackBench (56.7 vs 54.6). These are Baidu’s numbers; we did not run the models.

The card lists DeepSeek-V4-Flash-Base at 284B total and 13B activated parameters, 4.6% active by our arithmetic, against ERNIE’s 5.0%.

What the License Requires

Non-commercial use, including research and personal experimentation, is free with no notice. Commercial users at or below the US$50 million
revenue
line must send Baidu a one-time use notice and display “Powered by ERNIE” in at least one readily accessible place, such as the
product
’s documentation.

The license also bars using its outputs to train other systems. Section 5.5 reads: “You may not use the Licensed Materials, any Derivative Model, or any of their Outputs or results to improve any other artificial intelligence
model
or machine-learning system other than the Model or its Derivative Models”.

FOR BUILDERS
Non-commercial use is free; commercial use at or below US$50 million of related annual
revenue
needs
a one-time notice and a Powered by ERNIE display, per the license.

FOR LARGER COMPANIES
Above US$50 million, commercial use
needs
Baidu’s separate prior written authorization, per the license.

FOR READERS
